What to check before ordering

  1. Match the variant

    Compare the seller’s insole length for sneakers or chest width and garment length for hoodies and tees against an item you already own.

  2. Check agent eligibility

    If you plan to use Sugargoo, find the original marketplace link on the product page, then confirm that Sugargoo can accept that link and ship the item to your destination.

  3. Estimate the total

    Add domestic delivery, agent or payment fees, international shipping and any applicable taxes. For bulky shoes, check whether your shipping line charges by parcel dimensions.

  4. Review warehouse photos

    Where quality-control photos are available, match the colour and size label to your order. Check the seller’s return window before approving shipment.

Frequently asked questions

What is a Sugargoo spreadsheet?

It is a product discovery list that helps shoppers compare listings before checking whether they can order through Sugargoo. This independent guide uses HipobuyFind as the catalogue source.

Where do the buttons and product links go?

Both main buttons open the HipobuyFind homepage. Product names open the corresponding HipobuyFind listing so you can verify the item ID, variants and current details.

How should I compare CNY and USD prices?

Use the CNY column to compare catalogue prices. Use the USD column for budgeting, then check the exchange rate and charges displayed by your payment provider at checkout.
